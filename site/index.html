A Bangal point of view, served in South Kolkata.

EBELA OBELA's own Facebook identity describes it as a one-stop destination for Bangal food. That is stronger and more specific than generic “authentic Bengali” language, so the site lets that cultural cue lead.

Older editorial coverage described a home-kitchen-style operation with everyday Bengali dishes and Bangladeshi influences. We treat that as brand heritage rather than inventing a founder story the public record does not support.
